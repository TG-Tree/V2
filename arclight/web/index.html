What it will remember

A rolling 12-hour episodic buffer, indexed on the frames, purged continuously. Durable facts about you in a semantic store — every entry human-readable, source-timestamped, editable, deletable. Moments you pin, kept until you unpin them.

For how long

Episodes: 12 hours, then gone. Pins and facts: until you delete them. Everything lives on this device and your phone. Nothing is retained server-side.

What it will never do

Capture with the LED dark — the indicator is wired to the sensor's power rail, and the schematic is published. Store viewable frames at ambient tiers — embeddings only. Identify a stranger — ever, on-device or otherwise, even if you ask.
